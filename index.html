About This Project

KOMGRAF EduWorld is an immersive educational platform that brings iconic landmarks to life through interactive 3D visualization. Explore famous monuments from different eras and cultures with real-time lighting simulation and dual-view perspectives.

Explore Landmarks

Kaabah

The Kaaba is a building at the center of Islam's most important mosque, Al-Masjid al-Haram, in Mecca, Saudi Arabia. It is the most sacred site in Islam.

Great Pyramids

The Great Pyramids of Giza are monumental tombs built during Egypt's Old Kingdom. They stand as testaments to ancient Egyptian engineering and architecture.

🏺 Ancient Wonder 📍 Giza, Egypt 📅 2589-2504 BC
